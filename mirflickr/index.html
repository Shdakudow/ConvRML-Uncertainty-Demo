Calibration details

QUTCC trains a quantile function; calibration selects global lower and upper quantile inputs q. Im2im trains a center + bound predictor; calibration fits a per-pixel expansion map (K-RCPS) with a Hoeffding–Bentkus 90%-confidence certificate (α = 0.10, δ = 0.10).

3,000 calibration images are held out from the 2,000 test images. K-RCPS uses 10 images for optimization and the remaining 2,990 for the HB test.

Training settings & data

RGB images are 270 × 480. The split is 25,000 training, 2,000 validation, 2,000 test and 3,000 calibration pairs. MIRFLICKR lensless images captured with a DiffuserCam.
