What “harder” means here

A variant is labeled Harder than original when its average MAE across Im2im, ensemble-5, ensemble-10, and MC dropout increases by more than 10% relative to the original measurement. Individual model labels use the same rule for that model. This is a descriptive threshold for this example, not an intrinsic image-difficulty score or a statistical test.

This is a synthetic input-gain shift, not a physical exposure or sensor-noise simulation. Gains above 1 may clip the measurement. The ground truth is unchanged to test robustness to measurement scaling. RCPS factors were fitted on the original calibration distribution; coverage under this shift is measured, not guaranteed. One image cannot establish a general trend.

All shown models use epoch-50 weights. MC dropout uses p=0.05 and 30 passes, with matched random masks across brightness levels. QUTCC is excluded because its current adaptive run has not reached epoch 50.
