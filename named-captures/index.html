Preprocessing & calibration

The 12-bit sensor range is an assumption: these TIFFs use a 16-bit container, and the acquisition bit depth has not been confirmed. Inputs are divided by 4095, downsampled by a 4 × 4 area mean, and rounded to the training cache’s 8-bit-equivalent values. No per-image contrast stretching or rotation is applied.

Only the clean raw DiffuserCam Exp3 QUTCC and Im2im epoch-50 checkpoints are shown. Their original calibration is reused. Reconstruction and bounds are clipped to [0,1]. The calibration target is 90% on the original data distribution; coverage is not established for these unpaired external captures.

Saved inference ran on Apple MPS in float32. Mean width uses the saved float32 endpoints; PNGs are displayed at 8 bits per channel.
