Calibration at every saved milestone

QUTCC samples q uniformly during pinball-only training and uses 48 FiLM conditioning sites. Its frozen quantile inputs are selected by the paper’s adjusted empirical tail-risk search. Im2im trains all layers end to end with a center MSE term and two pinball endpoints; it fits independent lower and upper scale factors. These are paper-style empirical procedures, not Hoeffding–Bentkus RCPS; no δ confidence guarantee is claimed.

Nominal α = 0.10. The per-tail calibration budget is (0.10 − 0.90 / 3000) / 2 = 0.04985. Calibration uses native predictions; displayed clipped coverage can be higher. Failed calibration milestones are marked × and remain gaps in the calibrated line. After rechecking, any available native q = 0.05 / 0.95 result appears as a hollow diamond, explicitly labeled uncalibrated. It is not a valid calibrated interval.

Experiment settings and provenance

Raw arrays are RGB 270 × 480. Internal padding is cropped before loss and metric calculations. The original split contains 17,999 training, 2,000 validation, 2,000 test and 3,000 calibration pairs. Corrupted inputs are the exact reviewed PNG files; targets are unchanged. Cropping and patch rotations are sensor-domain stress tests, not a physically identical camera acquisition. Scrambling and the held-out noise families appear in the dataset-review controls; they are excluded from this training mixture.

Buttons load saved float32 inference arrays. No training or inference runs inside this browser. The first test examples follow the original manifest order; none are selected for visual quality. Negative interval widths, if present, are counted as crossings rather than silently sorted.
