Calibration method & fitted scales

QUTCC uses learned image-adaptive quantiles directly, with no RCPS, fitted scales, or calibration images. Its 90% coverage is a training target; the displayed coverage is measured on held-out data.

For im2im, ensemble and MC dropout, reconstruction stays unchanged. Each baseline starts with native bounds L₀ and U₀ around prediction μ. On 2,048 independent calibration images, separate lower and upper factors are fitted:

LB = clip(μ − sL × max(μ − L₀, 0.000001), 0, 1)
UB = clip(μ + sU × max(U₀ − μ, 0.000001), 0, 1)

Im2im learns its endpoints directly. QUTCC, when available, uses native image-dependent learned q endpoints from its epoch-20 checkpoint; the scale formulas above do not apply to QUTCC. Ensemble uses mean ± 1.64485 × standard deviation, including predicted noise variance and between-model variance. MC dropout uses the same Gaussian approximation with variance across 30 stochastic passes. For the baselines, RCPS allocates 5% miscoverage and δ=0.025 to each tail; overall target α=0.10 and confidence budget δ=0.05. This targets average pixel/channel coverage, not 90% for every image.

How to interpret this comparison

Examples marked Clipped [0,1] use clipped reconstructions and model-specific bounds for all image metrics, maps and pixel values. Validation curves and displayed test metrics use clipping to [0,1]. Per-image metrics appear below each model. Coverage is the fraction of RGB channel values inside calibrated intervals, not a guarantee that each whole image is covered.

Samples are saved inference results. The buttons select another test example; they do not train or run models in the browser. Quantile curves and interpolated quantile images are intentionally omitted from this comparison.
