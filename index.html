Experiment settings & interpretation

Full ConvRML, RGB 256×256. Original dataset split: 20,000 training / 2,500 validation / 2,500 test. This viewer uses the first 20 shared test entries. Fixed head: 50 reconstruction epochs, then 50 fresh head epochs. Other models: 50 epochs each; ensemble has five members. Training budgets are unequal.

Q-conditioned: direct network queries at the five quantiles. Ensemble: quantiles of the equally weighted five-Gaussian mixture, including predicted member variances. MC dropout: empirical quantiles from 30 new reproducible dropout draws. The ordinary reconstruction is displayed separately from q = .50.

Calibrated interval maps reuse factors fitted on 2,048 separate images at a 90% target. The curves are uncalibrated and need not reproduce those intervals. Coverage is empirical pixel-channel coverage, not a guarantee for every image. MC results may differ slightly from the earlier report because this viewer uses a new set of draws.

Displayed images are clipped to [0,1]; metrics use unclipped predictions. Arrays are stored at float16 precision for browsing; metrics are calculated before that conversion. Heatmap scale saturates at the pooled 99.5th percentile. Lower quantile crossings are shown as warnings, not silently repaired.
